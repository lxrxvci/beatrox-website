End-to-End Drone Production

From initial concept and 3D pre-visualization to FAA coordination, permitting, on-site setup, and live show operations, Beatrox manages the entire drone show production pipeline. Every formation is custom-designed to your brand, your story, and your event timeline.

FAA Permitting & Compliance

Every drone light show requires FAA coordination. Beatrox handles the entire regulatory process — including Part 107 waivers, airspace authorizations, and NOTAM (Notice to Airmen) filings. We work directly with the FAA to secure necessary permissions for both outdoor and indoor shows, ensuring full compliance with federal aviation regulations. Our team includes certified remote pilots and we maintain comprehensive liability insurance for all operations.

Indoor vs Outdoor Capabilities

Drone shows work in both environments with different considerations. Outdoor shows offer the largest scale — hundreds of drones across wide-open skies with long viewing distances. Indoor shows use specialized drones with protective enclosures and smaller propellers, operating safely in arenas, convention halls, and even atriums. Indoor shows require unique FAA approvals and venue coordination. We assess your space and recommend the optimal configuration for maximum impact and safety.

Weather Policy

Weather is a critical factor in outdoor drone operations. We monitor forecasts beginning 72 hours before your event and make go/no-go decisions based on wind speed, precipitation, and temperature thresholds. Generally, shows proceed in light rain and moderate winds up to 20 mph. Heavy rain, lightning, or sustained winds above 25 mph may require postponement or rescheduling. We build contingency plans into every production and communicate clearly with clients as conditions develop.

Designing a Show That Reads From the Ground

A drone show is a billboard the size of a skyline — but only if it's designed for the audience's vantage point. We choreograph formations around your primary viewing area, hold key brand moments long enough to photograph, and pace transitions so the sky never goes quiet. Logos and text are rendered in perspective-corrected formations so they read clearly from the crowd, and color palettes are chosen against your sky conditions — deep saturation for urban light glow, high-brightness whites for dark rural skies. Every design is reviewed with you in 3D pre-visualization before a single waiver is filed.

Frequently Asked Questions

+How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 4–6 weeks in advance for standard rentals and 8–12 weeks for custom productions. Last-minute requests are often accommodated depending on availability.

+Do you travel outside Portland?

Absolutely. While we're headquartered in Portland, OR, we regularly serve clients across the West Coast and nationwide for touring and large-scale events.

+What's included in the rental?

Every rental includes delivery, setup, on-site technical support, and strike. Custom packages can also include content design, pre-visualization, and dedicated project management.

+How many drones can you fly at once?

We can operate shows ranging from 50 drones for intimate settings to 500+ drones for large-scale spectacles. The ideal count depends on your venue, viewing distance, and creative goals.

+What happens if the weather is bad?

We monitor conditions 72 hours out and will recommend postponement if wind, rain, or lightning exceed safe thresholds. We build contingency plans into every outdoor production and communicate with you throughout.
