What is Audio Production?

Audio production is the engineering discipline behind what an audience hears — system design, tuning, mixing, and the hundred real-time decisions that keep a show sounding effortless — and our Portland, Oregon audio team delivers it for concerts and events nationwide. It's distinct from equipment rental: the same PA can sound world-class or amateur depending on who designs the coverage, rings out the room, and stands at the console. You need dedicated audio production whenever the show has stakes. A festival set where the artist's mix has to translate to a field of ten thousand, a keynote where the CEO's words have to reach the back row without a hint of feedback, a livestream where the broadcast mix is the only one that matters. Each demands engineers who've done it under pressure. Beatrox staffs audio the way tours do: an A1 at front of house owning the room mix, an A2 managing monitors and wireless, systems tuned before doors, and RF coordinated so nothing drops mid-chorus. The audience never thinks about audio — which is exactly the point.

What BEATROX Provides

Beatrox provides complete live audio production: system design and tuning, FOH and monitor engineering, RF coordination, playback and multitrack systems, and audio networking — with touring-grade engineers in every seat. We spec and tune the PA to the venue, ring out monitor worlds before the artist arrives, and manage the show from soundcheck to encore. For the Infinite Playlist Tour with Amazon Music Live, our audio teams supported live performances across two major festival environments — Outside Lands 2023 in San Francisco and Stagecoach 2023 in Indio — including sets by artists like Mariah the Scientist, where festival RF congestion and tight changeovers make disciplined frequency coordination and line checks non-negotiable. For Create Our Future with Urban Outfitters x Adidas at Mack Sennett Studio, we engineered audio for multiple live performances including Phantogram, with the mix holding clarity against a 270-degree projection-mapped Cyclone Wall. For BuzzFeed's NewFronts at Moynihan Station, audio production supported a full presentation program in a cavernous, reverberant hall. And for AKU World in New York, live musical performances ran synced to the room's projection and lighting systems — audio driving the energy of a fully reactive environment.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What's the difference between audio production and sound equipment rental?

Rental is the gear; production is the outcome. Audio production means engineers own the result — system design, tuning, mixing, RF coordination, and troubleshooting live. Our rentals can include engineers, and our production engagements always do.

+Can you handle an artist's rider?

Yes. We advance every show against the rider — console, microphones, monitor configuration, and input list — and flag substitutions early. Our engineers have mixed to major-tour riders at festival scale.

+How do you deal with wireless interference at festivals?

Professional RF coordination: we scan the spectrum on site, assign clear frequencies across every mic and IEM, and monitor for interference throughout the show. In congested environments like Outside Lands or Midtown Manhattan, this is the difference between a clean show and dropped channels.

+Do you mix for livestream and broadcast as well as the room?

Yes. We run dedicated broadcast mixes where the stream is the primary audience — as we did for the official festival livestream integration at Stagecoach for Amazon Music Live — with the FOH and broadcast worlds engineered independently.
