What is Engineering Certification?

Engineering certification is the formal verification that a structure is safe to build, suspend, and stand in front of an audience — a service Beatrox coordinates from Portland, Oregon for event structures nationwide. A licensed engineer reviews the design — its materials, connections, loads, and the forces it will see from rigging, crowds, weather, and its own weight — and stamps drawings that venues, fire marshals, and insurers require before they'll let anything go up. You need engineering certification whenever a build goes beyond off-the-shelf: custom-fabricated scenic, flown elements, tall freestanding structures, outdoor festival builds, or anything a venue's operations team asks questions about. Many venues and municipalities won't approve a load-in without stamped documents, and discovering that late is one of the most expensive surprises in production. Certification isn't a hurdle to clear — it's design input. When engineering thinking starts early, structures get lighter, rigging gets simpler, and approvals get faster.

Frequently Asked Questions

+Does every custom build need an engineering stamp?

Not every one — but most venues require stamped documents for flown elements, tall freestanding structures, and anything over or near an audience. We'll tell you early what's required for your specific venue and jurisdiction.

+How long does engineering certification take?

It depends on complexity, but plan on two to four weeks from final drawings to stamped documents. Starting engineering review during design development — not after fabrication — is the single biggest schedule saver.

+Can you certify a structure designed by someone else?

Yes. We regularly take designs from agencies and fabrication shops, develop the calculation and drawing packages, and coordinate the stamping with licensed engineers.

+Do you handle outdoor and wind-load certification?

We do. Outdoor builds — like festival structures — require wind and environmental load analysis, and we prepare those calculations as part of the certification package.
