What is Permanent Installation?

A permanent installation is an experience built to live in a space for years rather than days — a theatre, an architectural media feature, a branded environment — work Beatrox delivers from Portland, Oregon for venues and brands across the country. It demands a fundamentally different mindset than event production: every material, mount, and system is chosen for longevity, serviceability, and daily public use. You need a permanent installation partner when a space itself is the attraction — when the technology has to survive thousands of operating hours, be maintainable by local staff, and still look intentional a decade from now. The work spans structural engineering, media systems, environmental hardening, and the documentation that keeps it all running. The stakes are higher because there's no strike coming to rescue a mistake. Permanent work has to be right on day one and stay right — which is why it belongs with a team that has deployed media systems on some of the most demanding architectural canvases in the world.

What BEATROX Provides

Beatrox provides end-to-end permanent installation delivery: site assessment, structural and systems design, fabrication, large-scale media deployment, commissioning, calibration, and handover documentation. We engineer for serviceability from the start — access panels, replaceable components, and maintenance plans — so the installation's tenth year looks like its first. Our deployment pedigree includes DUBAI 360: Spherical Projection Theatre at Dubai Mall, delivered for DUBAI 360 and Obscura Digital — a spherical projection theatre environment requiring precise system deployment, content integration, and software in one of the world's highest-traffic retail destinations. For PROJECTING CHANGE: Racing Extinction, produced with the Oceanic Preservation Society and Obscura Digital, Beatrox was part of the team deploying large-scale projection mapping onto architectural landmarks including the Empire State Building, the United Nations in New York, and the Vatican in Rome — work where engineering precision and institutional coordination were non-negotiable. And Destination, our 40-foot interactive canvas structure for Journey's x Adidas in San Diego, shows the structural side of the craft: a towering engineered build with integrated mechanical and control systems designed to operate continuously under public use.

Frequently Asked Questions

+How is a permanent installation different from an event build?

Everything is engineered for longevity: materials rated for years of use, systems designed for serviceability, and documentation that lets local teams maintain it. There's no strike date to absorb mistakes — it has to be right from day one.

+Can you install in high-traffic public venues?

Yes. Our deployments include Dubai Mall and architectural landmarks like the Empire State Building and the Vatican — environments with strict access, security, and coordination requirements.

+Do you provide maintenance after installation?

We offer ongoing system maintenance and support, and every installation ships with documentation and training so your team or ours can keep it running for years.

+Do you work internationally?

We do. Our installation and deployment work has taken us from Dubai to Rome, and we manage the logistics, coordination, and compliance that international projects require.
