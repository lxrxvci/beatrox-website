What is Drafting & Detail Drawings?

Drafting is how an idea becomes a set of instructions — and from our Portland, Oregon studio, Beatrox produces the detail drawings that keep experiential builds across the country on the same page. In experiential production, detail drawings are the shared language between designers, fabricators, engineers, riggers, and venue staff — dimensioned plans, sections, elevations, and shop drawings that define exactly what gets built, where it goes, and how it fits together. You need professional drafting whenever something has to be fabricated, approved, or coordinated across departments. A scenic wall that looks simple in a render can hide dozens of decisions: material thicknesses, connection details, cable paths, access panels, sightline clearances. Without drawings, those decisions get made on the shop floor or on site — the two most expensive places to make them. Good drafting also protects the creative. When every vendor works from the same accurate documents, the finished build matches the approved design, change orders shrink, and load-in stops being a negotiation.

What BEATROX Provides

BEATROX produces full drafting packages in industry-standard CAD workflows: dimensioned floor plans, sections, elevations, fabrication shop drawings with material and hardware callouts, and technical plots for lighting, rigging, and AV. Because our drafters work alongside our fabricators and technical directors, the drawings reflect how things are actually built — not just how they look. Our drawings have carried real projects through approval and build. For Netflix's El Camino Comic-Con exhibition in New York, detailed documentation kept a complex exhibition build coordinated across departments. On the FLIR Interactive History Wall — a permanent installation at FLIR's Washington DC headquarters with ultra wide-format displays, LIDAR sensors, and a media server — precise placement and integration drawings were essential to a clean, low-maintenance result. And for Adidas' MyShelter launch, we drafted an immersive forest environment into a narrow Manhattan bodega, resolving how a hidden short-throw projector, scenic canopy, lighting, and signage could coexist in a footprint with no room for error.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What software do you draft in?

We work in standard CAD and 3D modeling tools — including Rhino3D and SolidWorks for complex fabrication geometry — and deliver files in formats your engineers, shops, and venues can open.

+Can you draft from an existing concept or render?

Yes. We regularly take creative renders, sketches, or reference models from design agencies and develop them into dimensioned, buildable construction documents.

+Do your drawings support engineering certification?

They do. Our drafting packages are produced with engineering review in mind — loads, connections, and materials clearly documented so a licensed engineer can evaluate and stamp them efficiently.

+Do I need drafting if my event is small?

If anything is being custom-built, suspended, or submitted to a venue for approval, yes. Even a simple drawing set prevents the on-site improvising that burns budget and time.
