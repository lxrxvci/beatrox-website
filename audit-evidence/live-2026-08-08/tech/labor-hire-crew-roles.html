What is Labor Hire for Events?

Labor hire is how productions get the specialist crew they need without carrying a full-time staff — and Beatrox supplies vetted event crew from Portland, Oregon to shows nationwide. Live events run on defined roles — the TD owns the technical systems, the PM owns schedule and budget, the A1 mixes front of house while the A2 runs monitors and RF, the V1 drives the screens while the V2 manages playback and switching, and the L1 designs and calls the lighting with the L2 on programming and focus. Each role is a career skill, and shows succeed or fail on who's sitting in those chairs. The challenge for most producers is finding people who are genuinely good — not just available. A resume full of acronyms doesn't tell you how someone behaves at hour fourteen of a problem load-in, or whether they can hold a show together when a playback system hiccups mid-keynote. That's what a labor partner solves: vetted professionals, matched to the actual demands of your show, booked under one accountable umbrella. You get the right number of the right people — no more, no less — plus the supervision and insurance structure that comes with an established production company.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What's the difference between A1/A2, V1/V2, and L1/L2?

The 1s lead their department: the A1 mixes front of house, the V1 runs the main video system, the L1 designs and calls lighting. The 2s support them — A2 handles monitors and wireless, V2 runs playback and switching, L2 programs and focuses fixtures. Bigger shows need both layers.

+Can I hire just one specialist instead of a full crew?

Yes. Single-role hires are common — a TD for a complex install, an A1 for a keynote, a media server operator for a playback-heavy show. We'll match the person to the actual technical demands, not just the title.

+How far ahead should I book crew?

Two to four weeks is comfortable for most shows; large crews and peak dates (festival season, major sporting events) benefit from more. We can often cover short-notice requests, but the best-fit specialists book first.

+Do your crew members travel?

They do. We staff productions nationwide and internationally, and we handle the travel logistics so your crew arrives briefed and on schedule.
