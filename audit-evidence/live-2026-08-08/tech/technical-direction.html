What is Technical Direction?

Technical direction is the discipline of turning a creative concept into a working production — a discipline Beatrox provides from Portland, Oregon for events and experiential builds nationwide. A technical director (TD) sits between the design team and the build crew, owning every technical system on a project — audio, video, lighting, projection, rigging, power, networking, and interactive technology — and making sure they all work together, on budget, on schedule, and safely. You need technical direction whenever a project has more moving parts than one person can track: multiple vendors, custom-fabricated elements, complex signal flow, or a venue with real constraints. The TD reads the drawings, questions the assumptions, coordinates the departments, and makes the hundreds of small decisions that determine whether load-in takes eight hours or eighteen. Good technical direction is invisible when it works. The show opens on time, the systems behave, and the creative team never has to think about the infrastructure underneath their vision. That invisibility is earned in pre-production — long before the first truck arrives.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What's the difference between a technical director and a production manager?

The production manager owns schedule, budget, and logistics. The technical director owns the systems — how audio, video, lighting, rigging, and interactive tech are designed, integrated, and operated. On complex shows you need both, and they work as a pair.

+When should we bring a technical director into the project?

As early as possible — ideally at concept stage. A TD involved early can flag unbuildable ideas, right-size the technical budget, and shape the design around the venue before money is spent on revisions.

+Can you provide technical direction without handling the full production?

Yes. We frequently embed a technical director into a client's existing production team, leading the technical departments while your producers run the overall show.

+Do your technical directors travel?

Absolutely. We're based in Portland, OR, but our directors have led productions in New York, Los Angeles, Miami, Zurich, and beyond — wherever the show is.
