What is Event Planning & Logistics?

Event planning and logistics is the discipline that turns a creative concept into a functioning, on-schedule reality — a discipline Beatrox runs from Portland, Oregon for events across the country. It covers everything between the approved design and the last truck pulling away from the venue: production timelines, vendor contracts, budgets, freight, crew schedules, venue coordination, and the hundreds of small decisions that determine whether doors open on time. You need this layer of support whenever an event has more moving parts than one person can hold in their head — which, in experiential production, is almost always. Multi-room builds, live performances, interactive installations, and public-site activations each bring their own vendors, approvals, and dependencies, and they all have to land in the same window. Done well, logistics is invisible: guests experience a seamless event while a tightly managed machine runs behind it. Done poorly, it's the reason load-in runs four hours late. We treat planning as a creative enabler — the structure that lets ambitious ideas actually happen.

What BEATROX Provides

We act as the connective tissue across your entire production. Our team builds the master timeline and run-of-show, sources and contracts vendors, tracks budgets against real costs, and coordinates freight — from custom-crated scenic pieces to last-minute gear rentals. On site, we run load-in schedules, manage crew calls, and keep every department aligned through doors, show, and strike. This is how projects like AKU World came together: a multi-room immersive event for AKU + FTX in New York that combined a projection-mapped main room, an interactive subway lobby, a holographic merch room, live music, panel discussions, and a patio bar — one coordinated production, many interlocking schedules. For Run for the Oceans, we planned and delivered a custom shipping-container build to Domino Park in Brooklyn for Adidas and Parley Ocean School's annual 5k event. And on the Infinite Playlist Tour for Amazon Music Live, we executed two full festival activations in a single season — Outside Lands in San Francisco and Stagecoach in Indio — each with its own custom build and logistics plan.

Frequently Asked Questions

+How far in advance should we start planning?

For most experiential events we recommend engaging 8–12 weeks out; large festivals or multi-city programs benefit from even more runway. That said, we've pulled together complex productions on compressed timelines — reach out and we'll tell you honestly what's achievable.

+Do you handle shipping and freight for custom builds?

Yes. We design scenic elements with transport in mind, crate them for safe freight, and manage the carriers and delivery windows — including multi-stop programs like the Infinite Playlist Tour's back-to-back festival deployments.

+Can you work with vendors we already have under contract?

Absolutely. We integrate your existing vendors into the master schedule and coordinate them alongside any partners we bring in. You get one timeline and one point of accountability.

+What do you need from us to get started?

A date, a location (or shortlist), a rough budget range, and your creative goals. From there we build the plan and flag every decision that needs your input.
