What are Media Server & Playback Solutions?

Media servers are the engines that store, process, and play back video content across complex display systems — systems Beatrox designs, programs, and operates from Portland, Oregon for shows nationwide — warping and blending multiple projectors onto irregular surfaces, driving LED walls at native pixel maps, and keeping every screen in a venue synchronized to the frame. Playback solutions cover the whole chain: server hardware, software programming, content formatting, signal distribution, and live operation. You need a media server specialist whenever content has to land precisely on a surface that isn't a flat rectangle, whenever more than one display must move as one image, or whenever playback simply cannot fail in front of an audience. Mapping a building, wrapping a dome, or syncing a dozen screens to a musical performance is a playback engineering problem before it's a creative one. The difference between a good show and a flawless one is usually invisible: redundant servers, frame-accurate timecode, content tested at show resolution before load-in. That preparation is the service.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What media server platforms do you work with?

We work across professional playback platforms and real-time engines — including TouchDesigner-based systems — choosing the server architecture that fits your canvas, content, and budget rather than forcing one platform on every show.

+How do you handle playback failure during a live show?

Redundancy is designed in from the start: mirrored backup servers, automatic or instant manual failover, and signal paths that keep the audience from ever seeing a fault. We rehearse the failure scenarios, not just the show.

+Can you map onto unusual surfaces or architecture?

Yes — irregular architecture, curved and spherical surfaces, and multi-projector blends are our core work. We pre-visualize against accurate venue models, then align and converge on site for pixel-accurate results.

+Do you supply the media server hardware?

We can. We specify, source, and configure the servers as part of the system, or program and operate on hardware you already own — whichever fits the production.
