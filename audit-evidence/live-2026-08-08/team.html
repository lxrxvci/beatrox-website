The Team

Our Team

We believe in the spirit of collaboration and foster positivity. We are agile and ready to respond with an established network of industry partners.

Nathan Jenkins · Founder & CEO / Creative Technologist

Founder and CEO of Beatrox. With over 20 years of experience in the industry, Nathan's expertise spans Audio Visual Design and Integration, Technical Direction, Lighting Design, and Sound Design. He brings a rare combination of creative vision and deep technical knowledge to every project.

Joey Paulekas · Touring & Fabrication

Whether it's Tour Manager, Art Director, Scenic Carpenter, or Production Manager, Joey has done it all. He has worked for global brands and has toured the world with renowned acts including Odesza and Emancipator, bringing a world-class perspective to every project.

Eric Mintzer · Creative Technologist

A technologist and music lover who has developed award-winning systems for projection mapping, sensor automation, and media control. Past companies include Startup Shell and Pamble. Eric is also the current Founder and CEO of ImagineX, pushing the boundaries of what interactive media can do.

Alan Curtiss · Video Production & LED Technician

Owner of Sky Lites Drone Shows and a core member of the Beatrox team. Alan has spent many years producing and managing high-profile technical productions with a wide variety of skills — mastering video production both behind the camera and on the screens. His specialties include Drone Operations, VJ/Media Server operation, and LED Technician work.

Desiree Daggy · 3D & Environmental Design

A 3D animator and Environmental Designer with expertise spanning character rigging to real-time environments. Desiree brings deep creative and technical skills from years of experience working in the events and entertainment industries, delivering stunning visual worlds for every project.

Joshua Vallejos · Audio Engineer / Production Manager

An Audio Engineer with over 15 years of experience spanning touring and live sound, studio engineering, music production, mix and mastering, and sound design for games and TV commercials. Whether on-stage or in the studio, Josh delivers with precision and passion.

Bryant Place · Creative Technologist

With over 13 years of global experience in the live entertainment and permanent installation industries, Bryant has a keen focus on merging technology and art. His international career gives him a unique perspective on how creative technology can transform any space or event.
