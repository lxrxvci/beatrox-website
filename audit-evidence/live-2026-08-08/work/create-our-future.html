The Experience

Create Our Future was an experiential event created for Adidas and Urban Outfitters that centered on live music, immersive visual production, and cutting-edge interactive technology. The centerpiece was a projection-mapped 270-degree Cyclone Wall — a self-climbing truss system that wrapped the stage in dynamic, full-surround visuals as a backdrop for multiple artist performances, including Phantogram. Guests were also invited to experience a custom-built VR station where they could draw and customize 3D digital avatars captured from a Microsoft Kinect Camera — bringing their personal creative expression into a digital world.

    Key Features

    • 270-degree projection-mapped Cyclone Wall with self-climbing truss system
    • Full-surround dynamic visual content for live performances
    • Multiple artist performances including Phantogram
    • Custom VR experience: guests draw and customize 3D digital avatars
    • Microsoft Kinect Camera for body-driven avatar capture
    • Unity + TouchDesigner pipeline for real-time content
    • Full lighting design and programming
    • Custom fabrication by Round House
