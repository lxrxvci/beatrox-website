About Us

The Team Behind the Tech

We are a team of imaginative storytellers who use technology to bring worlds to life.

Our Story

Beatrox was founded in Portland, Oregon with a single conviction: any idea, no matter how unconventional, is achievable. What started as a small collective of designers and technologists has grown into a full-service experiential design and event production company trusted by some of the world's most iconic brands. With over 20 years of combined industry experience, our team has designed and produced events across the United States and internationally — from immersive retail activations for Adidas to large-scale stage fabrication for worldwide tours.
